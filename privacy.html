Privacy Policy

PrepQuizGuru

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Introduction

PrepQuizGuru is an online practice platform designed to help competitive examination aspirants improve their knowledge through multiple-choice questions and explanations.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how users can contact us regarding their personal information.

2. Information We Collect

When you use PrepQuizGuru through Telegram, we may collect the following information:

  • Telegram account information: Your Telegram user ID and, when available, your Telegram username and first name.
  • Usage information: Records of when you start the bot, open the Mini App, and access a subject.
  • Quiz performance information: Quiz completion records, subject, number of questions, answered questions, correct answers, incorrect answers, unanswered questions and calculated accuracy.
  • Activity timestamps: Dates and times associated with recorded activity.

We do not currently request or collect your telephone number through PrepQuizGuru.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and operate the quiz platform.
  • Record quiz attempts and completion statistics.
  • Understand how users interact with different subjects.
  • Measure user activity and returning usage.
  • Monitor platform performance and identify issues.
  • Improve the quality and functionality of PrepQuizGuru.

4. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

PrepQuizGuru uses third-party infrastructure to operate its services and store analytics information.

  • Telegram: Provides the bot and Mini App platform.
  • Render: Hosts the PrepQuizGuru backend service.
  • Neon: Provides the database used to store user and analytics records.

These providers process information as necessary to provide their respective services and are subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by the service providers described above to operate and maintain PrepQuizGuru.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the security and integrity of our services.

6. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable measures intended to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure.

No online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to operate the platform, maintain analytics records, resolve issues and meet applicable legal requirements.

7. Your Privacy Requests

If you want to ask about the personal information associated with your Telegram account, or request correction or deletion, contact us using the email address below.

We may need sufficient information to identify the relevant records and verify your request. We will review requests in accordance with applicable requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

PrepQuizGuru is intended for people using the platform for educational and examination preparation purposes. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that should not have occurred, please contact us so we can review the matter.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, data practices or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
