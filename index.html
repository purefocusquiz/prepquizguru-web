ABOUT US

About PrepQuizGuru

PrepQuizGuru is an online practice platform designed for competitive examination aspirants who want to strengthen their knowledge and improve their problem-solving skills.

We provide practice questions across four key areas:

  • General English
  • Aptitude
  • Reasoning
  • General Studies

Users can attempt multiple-choice questions, check their answers, understand explanations and review their performance.

Our goal is to make regular practice simple and accessible while helping learners develop conceptual understanding, accuracy and confidence.

PrepQuizGuru is an independent practice platform. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government examination board or recruiting authority. Practice questions are provided for learning purposes and are not guaranteed questions from future exams.
